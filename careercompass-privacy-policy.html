Privacy Policy for the CareerCompass Mobile App

This privacy policy ("Policy") describes how CareerCompass ("We", "us" or "our") collects, protects, and uses the personal data received from users ("User", "You", or "Your") of our mobile application ("Application" or "Service"). By using our Application, you hereby consent to our Policy and agree to its terms.

Information We Collect

When you access or use our Application, we may ask for access to certain features on your device:

Device Permissions:

  • Internet Access: The Application requires internet connectivity to sync your data with Firebase cloud storage and access AI-powered features.
  • Storage Access: We request access to your device's storage to save and export resumes and cover letters as PDF files.
  • Audio Recording: The Application requests access to your device's microphone for interview practice features. We only access your microphone when you use the voice recording practice feature.
  • Notifications: We request permission to send you notifications for goal reminders and career milestone alerts.
Note: You have the option to decline these permissions. However, certain features of the Application may not work if permissions are denied.

Personal Information We Store:

We collect and store personal information you provide directly to us when you use our Application:

  • Profile Information: Your name, email address, and professional details to personalize your experience.
  • Career Data: Career goals, skills, work experience, education, and professional achievements.
  • Resume & Cover Letter Content: Personal and professional information you input for document generation.
  • Interview Practice Data: Voice recordings for practice sessions (stored temporarily and automatically deleted after analysis).
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous data about how you use the app to improve our services.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve the Application's functionality:

  • Career Development: To provide personalized career guidance, goal tracking, and skill gap analysis.
  • Document Generation: To create customized resumes and cover letters based on your profile.
  • Interview Preparation: To provide AI-powered feedback on your interview practice sessions.
  • Progress Tracking: To monitor your career development journey and provide insights.
  • Service Improvement: To enhance app functionality and user experience.

We do not store audio recordings permanently on our servers. Voice recordings are processed for feedback generation and then automatically deleted.

Storage of Information

Your personal information is stored using Firebase Firestore, a secure cloud-based database provided by Google. We use this data to:

  • Enhance your user experience across devices
  • Personalize content and recommendations
  • Provide seamless synchronization of your career data
  • Ensure data backup and recovery capabilities

How We Protect Your Data

The security of your data is vital to us. We employ industry-standard security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Secure authentication through Firebase Auth
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Sharing of Your Personal Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and prevent fraud
  • With service providers who assist in app functionality (under strict confidentiality agreements)

Your Rights Over Your Information

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Update: Modify or correct your personal information at any time within the app
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Download your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions previously granted

Account Deletion Process

How to Delete Your Account:

  1. In-App Deletion: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account in the CareerCompass app
  2. Email Request: Send a deletion request to wait careercompass.support@hungrymonkey.dev with your registered email address

What Happens When You Delete Your Account:

  • All personal data, including profiles, resumes, and career goals, will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Voice recordings from interview practice are automatically deleted after each session
  • Anonymous usage analytics may be retained for service improvement but cannot be linked back to you
  • Some data may be retained if required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention)
Important: Account deletion is irreversible. Please export any data you wish to keep before requesting deletion.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Inactive Accounts: Data may be deleted after 2 years of inactivity
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer if required by law
  • Voice Recordings: Automatically deleted after processing (within 24 hours)

Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for anyone under the age of 13 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from Children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. If we discover that we have collected personal information from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page

We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
